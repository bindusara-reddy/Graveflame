## Play

Requires [Godot 4.3+](https://godotengine.org/) (tested with 4.7.2).

- Linux: `./play.sh` (uses the NVIDIA GPU and disables V-Sync when one is present).
- Otherwise: open `project.godot` in Godot and press **F5**.

## Controls

`A/D` move · `Space` jump · `J` blade · `Down+J` air slam · `K` lance · `Q` ignite · `Shift` dash · `S` parry · `F` flask · `E` interact · `Esc` pause

Gamepad supported. The full keyboard and gamepad map is under **Controls** in the main menu.

## Status

Playable development build with original procedural vector art and synthesized audio. Pacing and difficulty are still being playtested.
